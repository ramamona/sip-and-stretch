# Code of Conduct

Sip & Stretch adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its Code of Conduct. By participating in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) you agree to follow it.

## The short version

Drip is a friendly little droplet, and we'd like the community to feel the same way.

**Please do:**

- Be kind, patient, and welcoming, especially to first-time contributors.
- Respect different viewpoints, experience levels, and ways of working.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Own your mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.
- Keep contributions (including personality lines and jokes) family-friendly and inclusive.

**Please don't:**

- Harass, insult, demean, or make personal or political attacks.
- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Troll, derail, or otherwise act in ways that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately to the maintainers:

- open a **private report** through GitHub at <https://github.com/ramamona/sip-and-stretch/security/advisories/new> (it's only visible to maintainers), or
- contact the maintainer using the email listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ramamona).

All reports will be handled promptly and confidentially. Maintainers will follow the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) based on the impact of the behaviour.
